Black & white paper

Ink on paper, no tile colors. Games are told apart by line icons and halftone textures (dots = words, lines = logic, grid = arcade). Hit/near/miss use fill and hatching instead of green and yellow, which also makes them readable for colorblind players. Three versions: pure B&W, B&W with one ink accent, and how the accent version looks in dark mode.
